# Scope — Pet Appetite Detective

Status: PROVISIONAL. Must be regenerated/reviewed through Devpost Learn Skill Pack before submission.

## Problem
First-time pet parents struggle to notice patterns in food acceptance across a week.

## User and outcome
A pet parent records seven daily observations and gets a factual summary to discuss with a veterinarian.

## In scope
Single pet, appetite score (0–3), stool note, behavior note, local persistence, seven-day summary, synthetic demo data.

## Out of scope
Medical diagnosis, personalized diet advice, cloud accounts, payment, CAIOS integrations.

## Success
Record, refresh, view and export a seven-day report without network access.
